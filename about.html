Who We Are

Anniyappa Publications Profile

Founded with a vision to build academic collaboration, Anniyappa Publications facilitates editing, peer review, and electronic distribution of research papers.

Over the years, we have published thousands of scholarly journals, reference volumes, and educational textbooks. Our dual model combines high-quality journal publication services with robust educational internship training, equipping upcoming graduates with essential editing, design, research, and project management skills.

We pride ourselves on offering standard DOI indexing, professional editing assistance, and curriculum-mapped educational resources that ensure writers get global visibility and learners gain industry readiness.

Our Journey

History of Anniyappa Publications

Foundation

Established as a small academic publishing unit dedicated to promoting research literature in Computer Science and emerging technologies.

Growth Phase

Expanded to cover multi-disciplinary journals and reference books. Partnered with indexing bodies for global visibility and scholarly recognition.

Education Wing

Launched SB Institute collaboration and internship programs, training 1200+ students in editorial workflows and publishing technologies.

Global Reach

Today we serve authors from 25+ countries, maintain 10+ indexed journals, and continue expanding our digital publishing infrastructure.

Publishing Vision

To establish a premium, globally integrated academic publishing platform that elevates knowledge distribution, creates ethical scientific literature, and prepares young scholars to confidently face industry challenges.
